How it works

Describe first. Reveal later.

The game keeps the target hidden while players record sensory impressions, sketches, shapes, colors, movement, texture, temperature, and whatever else seems to arrive before analysis takes over.

01

Choose a target

A Judge secretly selects a card and keeps the image hidden from the Viewer.

02

Perceive

Write or sketch raw impressions. Describe what you sense without naming the target.

03

Reveal

Turn over the card and compare the session to the actual target image.

04

Learn the signal

Notice what was accurate, what was analytical overlay, and how your own perception tends to speak.
